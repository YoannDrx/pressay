## Description

Pressay turns your voice into text across the apps on your Mac.

Hold your shortcut, speak, then release. Pressay transcribes locally and inserts the result where you are writing. The Voice Bar clearly shows the listening state and the active processing route.

LOCAL DICTATION, NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED

• Unlimited local dictation on Apple silicon
• Works offline
• No account required to get started
• Downloadable Fast, Polyglot, and Precise models

A NATIVE MAC EXPERIENCE

• Configurable global shortcut
• Minimal or Live HUD
• Personal dictionary
• Optional local history
• Per-app profiles and output modes

PROCESSING ROUTES STAY EXPLICIT

Local processing remains the default. Apple Intelligence, your own API key, and Pressay Cloud are used only when you select a compatible feature. Pressay never silently falls back to the Cloud.

PRESSAY PRO

Pro unlocks advanced transformation commands, custom modes, per-app profiles, Apple Intelligence/BYOK, encrypted sync, and a Pressay Cloud allowance. Local dictation remains available on Free.

Some features require Microphone and Accessibility permissions. Pressay explains why before requesting them. Apple Intelligence availability depends on your Mac, macOS version, language, and region.
